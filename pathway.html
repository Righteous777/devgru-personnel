03 / TRAINING COMMAND

How to earn a qualification

01 — Check eligibility

Meet the minimum rank and any required earlier qualifications.

02 — Apply or enroll

Contact Training Command or use the relevant application form or training channel.

03 — Attend training

Complete instruction and any supervised OPERATOR simulation exercises.

04 — Pass evaluation

Demonstrate skill, communications, conduct and required game objectives.

05 — Approval

An authorized evaluator records the result and the approving trainer confirms it.

06 — Discord award

A staff member awards the matching role under QUALIFICATIONS. Member retains that qualification unless formally suspended or revoked.

06 / SELECTION

Tier 1 — earn your place

Tier 1 is a selective community pathway. It is intentionally more demanding than basic operator membership. This handbook distinguishes three achievements: being approved for selection, becoming Tier 1 qualified, and joining the active Tier 1 roster.

StageDiscord roleWhat it means
01 ApplyT1 ┃ Selection ApplicantEligible member submits a selection application.
02 SelectedT1 ┃ Selection CandidateApplication accepted; candidate may begin assessments.
03 QualifiedT1-900 ┃ Tier 1 Qualified OperatorAll selection components passed; qualification approved.
04 Unit assignmentT1 ┃ Unit MemberQualified member applies to and is accepted onto the active Tier 1 unit roster.

Selection eligibility — E-4 minimum

  • E-4 or higher (officers must meet the same qualification prerequisites).
  • Completed Green Team and OP-101 through OP-106.
  • Hold CQB-201, CQB-202, CQB-205 and CQB-207.
  • Hold at least one relevant SPEC certification.
  • Participated in at least three supervised official operations.
  • Satisfactory attendance, teamwork and conduct record.
  • Obtain Training Command approval for selection.

PASS THRESHOLD
Minimum 85/100 and zero critical rule or conduct violations. Unsuccessful applicants receive feedback and may request reassessment after retraining. Passing is never guaranteed merely by achieving E-4.

Join the active unit

  • Earn T1-900 ┃ Tier 1 Qualified Operator.
  • Submit a separate Tier 1 Unit Membership application with a preferred team and specialist role.
  • Provide your current rank, qualifications, recent operation participation and availability.
  • Complete an integration operation with current Tier 1 unit members.
  • Receive selection director recommendation and final unit command acceptance.
  • When accepted, receive T1 ┃ Unit Member and an assigned team role.

If the Tier 1 unit roster is full, a qualified member may remain T1-900 qualified without holding T1 ┃ Unit Member. This is not a loss of qualification.

Tier 1 squadron assignments: Red / REAPER, Gold / VIKING, Silver / SPECTER. Contact Training Command for applications.

08 / NEXT STEPS

Frequently asked questions

Can an E-3 earn certifications?

Yes. E-3 operators can complete core, beginner CQB, weapons and entry-level specialist courses.

Can I apply directly to Tier 1 at E-3?

No. The recommended minimum is E-4, plus core, CQB and specialist prerequisites.

Does passing Tier 1 mean I am on the active unit?

No. First earn T1-900, then apply separately for the T1 Unit Member roster.

Can a Tier 1 operator command a squad?

Not by qualification alone. Command duties follow rank and appointment requirements.

How do I teach Green Team?

Reach E-5, earn instructor qualification, complete supervised sessions and obtain appointment.

How do I teach CQB?

Reach E-5, earn the required CQB and instructor certificates, and pass a supervised teaching review.

Do medals replace certifications?

No. Medals recognize achievement; certifications record passed assessments.

Do I lose a qualification when promoted?

No. Earned qualifications remain unless formally suspended or revoked after review.

Who gives the Discord role?

An authorized administrator or personnel officer awards the role after documented approval.
